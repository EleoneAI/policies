01 Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to data minimization. We collect only what is essential for exam progress tracking:

Data Category Specific Data Points Why It Is Needed Storage Period
Identity & Authentication Full Name, Email Address, Password (salted & hashed) Account creation, multi-device synchronization, and secure password recovery. Until account deletion
Profile Metadata (Optional) Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, Place/City Age-specific eligibility reminders and personalized profile display. Until account deletion

What We Explicitly DO NOT Collect: We never request or inspect your device contacts, SMS, microphone, camera, photos, precise GPS coordinates, financial accounts, or device hardware identifiers (IMEI/MAC).

02 Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

We process your data strictly under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of Service: Storing your syllabus progress and computing revision reminders you scheduled.
  • User Consent: Sending requested password reset codes to your verified email address.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting cloud integrity and mitigating abusive automated traffic.

We never share, license, rent, or sell your personal data to commercial marketers, advertisers, or third-party profiling engines.

03 Cloud Architecture & Security Controls

Aspirants is engineered with multi-layered technical security safeguards:

  • Transport Security: All client-to-cloud communication enforces TLS 1.3 encryption. Unencrypted HTTP is strictly rejected.
  • Data Isolation: Every syllabus topic, milestone, and study entry is strictly bound to your authenticated user token. No other user can access or query your preparation data.
  • Token Security: Authentication tokens are stored using device-level secure keystores (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences) with PKCE OAuth 2.0 flow.

04 Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

You have full ownership and sovereignty over your preparation journey:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile Settings → DANGER ZONE → Delete Account. After a double-check confirmation, your active profile, subjects, chapters, topics, study logs, and active reminders are permanently erased from the live database.
  • Web Request Option: You can also request deletion through our dedicated Account Deletion Web Portal or by emailing support@eleone.io.

05 Your Legal Rights

Under the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and European Union GDPR, you possess full rights to access, rectify, or permanently erase your personal data at any time.
