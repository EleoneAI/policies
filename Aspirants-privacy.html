Privacy Policy for Aspirants

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by ELEONEIO™ SYSTEMS

Aspirants ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by ELEONEIO™ SYSTEMS, is dedicated to empowering students with preparation progress tracking while respecting their fundamental right to data privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Full Name, Email Address, optional Date of Birth, and Gender for account management and profile display.
  • Study Progress & Activity: Target exam names, syllabus topics marked complete, confidence ratings, and study hours logged.
  • We DO NOT Collect: Precise GPS location, contacts, phone calls, SMS, camera access, biometric identifiers, or advertising tracking IDs.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly to provide exam progress calculations, revision schedules, and personal countdown milestones. We do not sell, license, or monetize your personal preparation data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Security & Cloud Storage

All data exchanged between the Aspirants app and our cloud services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. User databases feature isolated row security boundaries ensuring only authenticated owners access their study logs.

4. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may permanently erase your account, exam selections, and all study records at any time directly inside the App (Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account) or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.

5. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding privacy practices, contact us at: support@eleone.io.
