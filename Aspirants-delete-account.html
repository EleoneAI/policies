RIGHT TO ERASURE

Account Deletion & Data Purge

In compliance with Google Play Developer User Data Policies and global privacy regulations (including India DPDP 2023 & EU GDPR), you have absolute ownership over your account. You can permanently wipe your profile and study records at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Erasure
Instant & Automatic
1

Open Aspirants on your Device

Ensure you are signed into the account you wish to delete.

2

Navigate to Profile Settings

Tap the menu icon (top left) → Select Profile Settings.

3

Access the Danger Zone

Scroll to the bottom of the screen to find the red DANGER ZONE card → Tap Delete Account.

4

Double-Check Confirmation

Read the warning summary, select an optional reason, and tap "Yes, Permanently Delete My Account".

✓

Immediate Purge Completed

All active study tables, topics, progress logs, and your profile are immediately destroyed from the active cloud database. You will be signed out instantly.

Method 2: Email Data Erasure Request
