Account Deletion & Data Erasure

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users of Aspirants (by ELEONEIO™ SYSTEMS) can delete their account and associated data through either of the methods below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Aspirants app on your device.
  2. Open the navigation drawer and tap Profile Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the DANGER ZONE card and tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm the final approval dialog.

Your active study records, syllabus topics, progress logs, and profile are immediately and permanently erased from the active database.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email from your registered address to support@eleone.io with the subject line "Delete My Account". Your request will be fulfilled within 48 business hours.
